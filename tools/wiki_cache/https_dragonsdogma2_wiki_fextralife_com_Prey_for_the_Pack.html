                      Prey for the Pack | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      Prey for the Pack
                      Quest Location Checkpoint Rest Town
                      NPC Pickup Morris from Morris's Apothecary
                      Requirements Reach Checkpoint Rest Town
                      Time Limited Yes
                      Quest Canceled Failed to reach Rodge in time
                      Unlocks n/a
                      Other NPCs Lutz, Timothy, Jacint, Alsa, Rodge
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes
                      Rewards 4000 XP
                      0 Dcp
                      x2 Miracle Roborant
                      11,000 G

                      Prey for the Pack is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). Prey for the Pack is affected by the passage of time. This quest involves helping Morris's grandson who was taken by a pack of wolves, so you must come to his aid immediately. 

                       

                      A pall hangs over the Checkpoint Rest Town, as fear for the grandson of the local apothecary lines every face. It falls to you to find the boy, and spare him a grizzly fate.

                       

                      How to Unlock Prey for the Pack Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      The Prey for the Pack is unlocked when you approach Morris' shop in Vernworth. Choose the dialogue option "Pledge your assistance" as you listen to the details of the conversation between Morris and Lutz. See Prey for the Pack on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Prey for the Pack Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This is a Time-Sensitive quest.
                      • Hurry to complete this quest as Rodge can die if the quest is unattended. 
                      • Do not rest when you are in the progress of this quest. The passage of time will result in Rodge's death.
                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes:
                        • Rodge is safely escorted back home
                        • You arrived too late to aid Rodge, or You found Rodge but failed to escort him back home safely.
                      • Follow the blue glowing flowers to find the entrance of the Putrid Cave.
                      • There have been reports of the quest being buggy or failing under certain conditions:
                        • One example is encountering an Oxcart while escorting Rodge back to town; using the Oxcart can cause the quest to glitch, preventing you from completing the quest.
                      • When escorting Rodge, make sure you return to town on foot and protect him throughout the journey. To avoid him wandering off or acting unpredictably, you can carry him, ensuring he stays on the right path and minimizing any risk of him moving erratically.
                      • This quest will fail if you don’t reach Rodge in time or disregard the passage of time. If this happens, the player will find that Rodge has already been killed by wolves and is no longer in the cave when they arrive.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Prey for the Pack Quest Walkthrough

                      Find Rodge and Go to his aid

                      Rodge, the grandson of the apothecary, has been attacked by a pack of wolves. After agreeing to help Morris, you'll be instructed to talk to the townsfolk to learn where Rodge was last seen and to find out where the wolves have taken him. Start by speaking with Lutz, who was just in conversation with Morris, and then head to the group of townspeople gathered outside The Sword and Staff.

                      You can gather information from three NPCs. First, there's Timothy, who wears a blue shirt and a round hat. Next, look for Alsa, a child dressed in a green dress and work boots. Finally, talk to an elderly woman named Jacint, who has a white dress and a cloth draped over her head. After conversing with them, a location leading to the Putrid Cave will be marked on your map.

                      You will learn 3 things about Rodge's location:

                      • It seems Rodge was by the cenotaph on the eastern side of town when he was set upon by wolves.
                      • According to Jacint, Rodge tends to a flowerbed on the outskirts of town.
                      • You learned that glowing flowers bloom at night in the area.

                      Follow the Guerco Mountain Road across the bridge where an oxcart is stationed. This route will take you to Rodge's location. You will navigate through a narrow river and pass a short tunnel. Be cautious, as harpies gather at the end of this tunnel. Continue on the path ahead, which will lead you to a campfire located above the Putrid Cave, where glowing flowers are present.

                      Again, it is not advised to rest as Rodge's life is on the line. Instead, go to the path on the lower side of the campfire where you see the Riftstone and a path that leads to the Putrid Cave. When you see the wooden bridge, this is the mark that you are in the right location.

                      Escort Rodge to the Checkpoint Rest Town

                      At this point, you MUST safely escort Rodge back to Checkpoint Rest Town. In the meantime, avoid exploring other locations and focus on getting back to the town safely as the quest can still fail. During our playthrough, it was dark when we found Rodge and we didn't stop to rest, we traveled back to town as fast as we could. The sun was up when we reached Checkpoint Rest Town and took Rodge to reunite him with Morris. After this short conversation, the Prey for the Pack will be marked as completed.

                       

                      DD2 Prey for the Pack Quest Objectives

                      • Find Rodge and go to his aid.
                      • Escort Rodge to the checkpoint rest town.
                      • Report back to Morris.
                      • Inform Morris of Rodge's wellbeing.

                       

                      Prey for the Pack Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. You found Rodge but failed to escort him home safely:

                      To the horror of the local townsfolk, Rodge, the apothecary's grandson, was dragged off by wolves. With the local soldiers unable or unwilling to act, you were sent to the lad's rescue; but while you managed to protect him from the beasts, you failed to see him safely home.

                      You were too late to aid Rodge:

                      To the horror of the local townsfolk, Rodge, the apothecary's grandson, was dragged off by wolves. With the local soldiers unable or unwilling to act, you were sent to the lad's rescue; yet arrived too late to save him, finding naught but his remains.

                      • 4,000 XP
                      • 8,000 G

                      You escorted Rodge back home safely:

                      To the horror of the local townsfolk, Rodge, the apothecary's grandson, was dragged off by wolves. With the local soldiers unable or unwilling to act, you were sent to the lad's rescue, and managed to escort him safely out of the beasts' den and all the way back home.

                      You escorted Rodge back home safely - Prey for the Pack Rewards:

                      • 4,000 XP
                      • x2 Miracle Roborant
                      • 11,000 G

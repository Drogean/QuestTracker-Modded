                      A Game of Wits | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      A Game of Wits
                      Quest Location Mountain Shrine
                      NPC Pickup Sphinx
                      Requirements Reach the Mountain Shrine
                      Time Limited No
                      Quest Canceled Unknown
                      Unlocks When successful with the first 5 riddles, you will unlock the 2nd location of the Sphinx
                      Other NPCs Dante, or Vergil and a beloved NPC (Ulrika is an option)
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes
                      Rewards (Final Reward for defeating the Sphinx):
                      Eternal Wakestone

                      A Game of Wits is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). A Game of Wits is not affected by the passage of time. This quest requires you to find the Sphinx's location and start a battle of wits where the Arisen must answer all 10 Riddles correctly to earn various rewards.

                       

                      You have encountered the legendary sphinx, a beast of intellect who seeks to pit its riddles against sage and warrior alike. How will you fare against it?

                       

                      How to Unlock A Game of Wits Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      You can pick up A Game of Wits after reaching the location of the Sphinx at the Mountain Shrine. You will find this north of the Checkpoint Rest Town with the path heavily guarded by strong enemies. See A Game of Wits on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 A Game of Wits Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes: The beast abandoned your game, or You solved the Sphinx's riddles
                      • This quest requires you to locate the Sphinx's location in the Mountain Shrine
                      • This quest is canceled if you don't pick it up before completing the mission Legacy.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 A Game of Wits Quest Walkthrough

                      Locate the Sphinx: Mountain Shrine

                      A Sphinx in Greek mythology is a figure that represents a spiritual guardian with a human's head, a lion's body, and an eagle's wing. In the World of Dragon's Dogma, the Sphinx possesses similar features and serves as a guardian protecting 5 different chests. Each treasure chest can be solved by doing its specific puzzle and solving the riddles. To begin, the players must set out on a journey to discover the Sphinx's lair. To start this quest, you first need to locate the Sphinx. Ideally, you will travel from Checkpoint Rest Town, found on the western side of the map. Then, follow the outlined path in the image below. You will travel to the Northern area and pass through the Ancient Battleground, an open area below the first entry point, Worldsend Cavern. A large portion of this area is a castle rubble that will lead to the entrance of the Worldsend Cavern. From there, follow the newly revealed path that will take you to the Shrine Corridor. You will find the entrance to the Mountain Shrine directly on top by the other side using the stairs. The legendary Sphinx is waiting for you at the top.

                       

                      Answer the Sphinx's Riddles (Part 1)

                      NOTE: You're only given 1 try to answer per riddle. If you give the Sphinx a wrong answer, it will lock you out from earning the trophy from that playthrough and the Sphinx will abandon your game. You will have to redo your progress on another playthrough. It is highly recommended to create save files or backups before you approach the Sphinx to answer. This way, you are guaranteeing a second chance for yourself if you happen to give the wrong answer. 

                      This section is dedicated to all the riddles from the Sphinx at her first location in Mountain Shrine. 

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #1: The Riddle of Eyes DD2

                       “Our eyes are our allies, yet oft do they betray, for eyes tell lies, so I advise, and thence do lead astray. Yet how will your eyes advise you? Venture through yonder door, and retrieve that which of greatest value.”

                      After getting the first Riddle from the Sphinx, you will be told to travel to a cave and look for the item that you think is of the greatest value. Inside this cave, you will navigate traps, defeat goblins, and possibly encounter an ogre. There are also treasure chests within the cave where you can get valuable items from.

                      One of the treasure chests can be located right above the door when you enter the cave, inside the chest is a Sealing Phial, this is the item that you will have to give to the Sphinx. To reach the chest, there is a path going up to the left if you're facing the door. You will be rewarded with a Wakestone upon completing the riddle.

                      NOTE: If you consume the Sealing Phial, you will not be able to solve this riddle.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #2: The Riddle of Madness DD2

                      "Love is as twin to madness, they say. They are bound fast as night is to day. So bring forth your most beloved one to me, that I might gauge the depth of your insanity."

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, you will have to bring someone who is your most beloved which is a character you have romanced. For the simplest solution, your Main Pawn can be picked up, placed upon the pedestal, and used as your answer to the riddle, which is completely accepted by the Sphinx. If you wish to bring your true beloved, you can use the Sealing Phial which allows you to pick up and pocket a character in your inventory and then let them out by the Sphinx. Another option is to carry the character, and use a Ferrystone whilst they are in your arms, if you have placed a Portcrystal near the Sphinx. 

                      Ulrika is confirmed to work on this riddle if you have romanced her. Glyndwr as well, regardless of gender. So long as the NPC blushes when spoken to, they have enough Affinity to be romanced. If you bring more than one person you have romanced, you will still complete the riddle. However, they will begin to kill each other until only your true love remains. You will be rewarded with a Portcrystal upon completing the riddle. In NG+, you will be given a Partcrystal, the Portcrystal forgery.

                      Sphinx Riddle #3: The Riddle of Wisdom DD2

                      "The parent knows the child, yet the reverse is far from true. The child knows not the parent: such is the parent's due. I am a lost child; for kinship do I yearn. So bring to me my "Parent". that might better learn."

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, you will have to enter the rift and find a pawn called "SphinxFather" who is a Capcom-made pawn for you. Bring him to the Sphinx to complete the riddle, you will also be rewarded with 1200 RC for completing this riddle.

                      • NOTE: When at a riftstone, select "View Linked Riftstones" and select "Riftstone of Fellowship" there you'll find either a Father, Mother, or Parent you can use.
                      • Any pawn with their Moniker being "SphinxFather", "SphinxMother" or "SphinxParent" will work, you do not have to use an official Capcom pawn.
                      • Should you chose any of the aforementioned monikes as your Main Pawn's moniker, you can use them as the answer of this riddie as well.
                      • The only way you can change you Pawn's moniker is to enter New Game Plus.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #4: The Riddle of Conviction DD2

                      "Life is an enigma – a lender of mortal debt. Yet lighter pack makes fleeter foot and challenge nimbly met. So grant to me what you most prize, and thence elude your pondrous demise."

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, you can give any item to the Sphinx and it will act as if it will not return it to you. When actually, it will duplicate the item it will give you as a reward.

                      NOTE: Giving a Portcrystal to the Sphinx here can be useful in the early parts of the game. One can also give the Jadeite Orb to the Sphinx to give Everard and Offulve the genuine treasure to complete the Quest: Hunt for the Jadeite Orb, this way they don't have to use forgeries and can save the small forgery fee.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #5: The Riddle of Rumination DD2

                      "It's ever the first we keep fond in our breast, and ever the first that eclipses the rest. You know of the Seeker's Tokens, I trust? Those keepsakes of a fondly remembered journey? Yet where was it that you found your first? Retrace your steps, if you can – you might make a new discovery. Seven days, shall we say, ere you return? May your journey be a pleasant one."

                       

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, you will have to go back to the place where you picked up your first Seeker's Token. The Finder's Token will be laying on the ground there. If you start a New Game Plus, it will be where the first Seeker's Token you picked up in that New Game Plus was. If you haven't collected any Seeker's Token's yet, it has been reported that it will appear next to the next Seeker's Token you pick up. If you've already collected all Seeker's Token's in a previous game, it has been reported that the Finder's Token will appear right at your feet in front of the Sphinx when you begin the riddle.

                      NOTE: If you've found your first Seeker's Token right before speaking to the Sphinx, you may not be able to find the Finder's Token immediately afterwards. You will need to rest at an inn or dwelling first, then return to the spot.

                      Once you obtain it, head back to the Sphinx and present the item to it. You will be rewarded with a chest filled with 3x Ferrystones.

                       

                      Find Sphinx's Second Location: Frontier Shrine

                      After completing all 5 Riddles, the Sphinx will go to her second location in Frontier Shrine. You need to find her again to continue playing the rest of the riddles. If you can manage it, you can climb on the back of the Sphinx just as she leaves so you can get a free ride to its second location. This can require a great amount of stamina, but you can let go once on its back and stand on top of it, rather than drain your stamina until you inevitably fall off. Simply watch out once it enters the cave; it will began to bank upwards, and you'll need to grab on for a good while, or fall off.

                      Alternatively, you can go to the second location by following the river below the Checkpoint Rest Town and follow it to the west. This will lead you directly to the entrance of the Frontier Shrine. 

                       

                      Answer the Sphinx's Riddles (Part 2)

                      This section is dedicated to all the riddles from the Sphinx at her second location in Frontier Shrine.  

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #6: The Riddle of Reunion DD2

                      This riddle is solved automatically upon finding the Sphinx in its second location. There will be a chest on the left side that you can open and you will be rewarded with 100,000 Gold.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #7: The Riddle of Recollection DD2

                      "Now, questions beget questions, and I have one for you. How many riddles have you solved thus far? My memory fails me, you see. Remind me, and make it plain. Let yonder statues be your means. For every question aptly answered, bring one here before me.”

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, a set of stone statues will appear to your left. What you will need to do is count how many riddles you've been given by the Sphinx so far, whether you answered them correctly or not. The number corresponds to the number of glowing eyes on her wings when she put them up after asking this question. Then, move the same number of statues onto or next to the dais in front the Sphinx. You will be rewarded with an Unmaking Arrow upon completing this riddle.

                      NOTE: The order for riddles 7-10 may be randomized, so you might get this riddle later on in the game.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #8: The Riddle of Contest DD2

                      “Though ours is a battle of wits, tests of mettle are more to your strength. Is that not so? Come, try your arm – though he shall be your opponent, not I. However, I am not one to be amused by a simple duel. Bear this ring into battle, that I might gauge your true strength.”

                       

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, an enemy will spawn on the right. You will have to defeat this enemy to complete the riddle. However, as the sphinx forces you to wear the Ring of Derision, which causes all attacks to deal 1 damage, it will be substantially faster to simply pick up the enemy and throw them off the cliff. You will be rewarded the Ring of Ambition upon completing this riddle.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #9: The Riddle of Futility DD2

                      “How easily the mind unravels! Some say it’s pain that does it best, others darkness – but I say neither. Rather, I say a mind cannot be more soundly broken than when it beholds its earnest efforts laid low in a single stroke. Could your survive such despair? We shall see. Here I have an amphora destine for Ser Maurits in Battahl. Deliver it to him, but beware; it’s rather fragile.”

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, it will give you a vase that you will have to bring to a location called Mural Byway [See DD2 Map] without breaking it. Upon reaching it, there will be an NPC next to a Sphinx mural that will talk to you and you will automatically put down the vase after speaking with them. Return to the Sphinx to complete the riddle and claim the Eternal Bond Ring as your reward. 

                      While the player can attempt to physically carry the amphora across the map without being hit a single time, (and an Ox Cart from Rest Town is off the table, as the carefully laid to rest amphora will unceremoniously piss'a'dear upon arriving in Bakbattahl), an alternative way of solving the quest is to work smarter, not harder. You cannot use a Ferrystone with the amphora in hand, but as you likely know by now, you CAN use a Ferrystone with an NPC over your shoulder. Instead of carrying the vase, carry the quest NPC from Mural Byway (there is only one) and use a Ferrystone to teleport to a Portcrystal you've placed at the Frontier Shrine. The NPC has unique dialogue explicitly referencing the fact that you brought them there, and that you must have done so because the amphora was hidden there, which indicates that the developers likely intended this to be the solution.

                      NOTE: You will fail to complete the riddle if you break the vase so to prevent that, you can make a save file before going to the Sphinx for its riddles.

                       

                      Sphinx Riddle #10: The Riddle of Differentiation DD2

                      “So vast is the world, and full of life; you are but one of many. Indeed, in the grand scheme, we are as distinct from one another as pebbles on a beach – yet we do so love to extol our differences. But are these differences so great? If you believe so, this next task should prove exceedingly simple. I seek this man. If men are so distinct, I’m sure you’ll find him in a trice.”

                      After getting this riddle from the Sphinx, you will have to find a specific NPC, either Dante or Vergil, these 2 NPCs look alike but are found in different towns just wandering around.

                      Dante has straight hair and his scar goes downward from his right eyebrow down below his eye and can be located in Checkpoint Rest Town, on the Vermund side of the border. Vergil has curly hair and he has more scars on his right eye that go down sideways and can be located in Checkpoint Rest Town on the Battahl side of the border.

                      Once you find the needed NPC, you can carry them back to the Sphinx. The NPC may wiggle and struggle while you hold them, so the best approach might be to kill them, which will stop them wiggling. Bringing them back dead to Sphinx will also complete the quest, you can also resurrect them after. Another method is to place a Portcrystal at the Sphinx and then teleport back while carrying the NPC with a Ferrystone. You will be rewarded with a Whimsical Daydream.

                      NOTE: Make sure to have a save file before starting this riddle in case you are not sure if it is Dante or Vergil that you need.

                       

                      Dante
                      Vergil
                       

                       

                      Defeat the Sphinx

                      After completing all 10 Riddles, your next objective is to defeat the Sphinx. Initiate the fight by attacking the Sphinx and it will reveal having 4 Health Bars. Upon defeat, A Game of Wits will be marked as completed and the Sphinx will try to fly away. Make sure to shoot it down to earn gold rewards and obtain the Key of Sagacity which is used to unlock the huge golden chest of the Sphinx. The chest contains an Eternal Wakestone which will let you revive the dead in a certain area at once. 

                      • Fighting the Sphinx itself is a riddle and requires the player to focus on the non-human parts.
                      • Hit the Sphinx only on the animal parts so it won't fly away.
                      • You can defeat the Sphinx without using the Unmaking Arrow but you have to take this fight alone. 
                      • Pawns are unable to control where they land their attacks so it is recommended to keep them out of the fight. Use the command "Wait" to do this.
                      • The clue to how you can fight the Sphinx can be found on the mural and the vase during Riddle of Futility (or #9 of this guide)

                      NOTE: The sphinx has a tendency to fly off mid-fight, so the easiest way to guarantee a victory is to use the unmaking arrow you obtained from the Riddle of Recollection (or #7 of this guide)

                      NOTE: The sphinx will IMMEDIATELY start to fly away after finishing the last riddle. Make sure to be ready to attack it as soon as the last riddle's dialogue ends. The unmaking arrow only works after engaging the Sphinx and it's health bar appears. Shooting it with the arrow before the health bar appears will kill it and award you with key and gold but the quest won't be completed, leaving you unable to finish it. You can't climb onto the sphinx as it flies away like from the previous shrine.

                      • The Riddle of Madness

                      • The Riddle of Conviction

                      A Game of Wits Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. The beast abandoned your game:

                      Though you sought to challenge the riddling creature known as the sphinx, it found you lacking, and abandoned your game in favor of pursuing fresh prey for its wiles.

                      You answered the Sphinx's riddles correctly:

                      You challenged the riddling creature known as the sphinx, facing its trials with surpassing wisdom before besting it through sheer force of might. In doing so, not only did you earn the creature's esteem, but a fascinatingly unique key.

                      You answered the Sphinx's riddles correctly - A Game of Wits Rewards:

                      • x XP
                      • x Dcp
                      • Eternal Wakestone

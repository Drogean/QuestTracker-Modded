                      Every Rose Has Its Thorn is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). Every Rose Has Its Thorn is affected by the passage of time. It seems Wilhelmina is haunted by her past and has been plotting a vengeful plan to take down Allard. The Arisen offers his/her aid in finding pieces of evidence to prove the crimes of Allard.

                       

                      Wilhelmina, proprietress of the Myrmecoleon, is determined to exact vengeance on the man who wronged her—but what crime could possibly have inspired such hatred?

                       

                      How to Unlock Every Rose Has Its Thorn Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      Progress the main story by beating the quests "The Stolen Throne" and "An Unsettling Encounter". Speak to Wilhelmina a few times until she delivers several lines of dialogue at once, ending with "Here I am...". She may only deliver this dialogue if her affinity with you is high enough; you may need to speak to give her a gift once a day until she gives you the line (she likes Bunch of Flowers). Now, after she says this, rest at an inn or perform some other method to advance time outside of the Noble Quarter. Then go up the top floor of the Rose Chateau Bordelrie and you should find Rolf standing outside the room next to Wilhelmina's. If this still isn't the case for you after resting, other people have reported traveling out of the city and then going back to be able to start this quest. Go inside Wilhelmina's room and use the peephole to eavesdrop on Wilhelmina and Allard. After the cutscene, you'll speak to Wilhelmina. Choose "Offer your assistance" to unlock the quest. Choosing to dissuade her will permanently lock you out of starting the quest, and Wilhelmina will say that she doesn't want to speak to you anymore. See Every Rose Has Its Thorn on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Every Rose Has Its Thorn Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This is a Time-Sensitive quest.
                      • This quest unlocks a Romance scene with Wilhelmina if you choose the best outcome for this quest, and make sure to complete the quest during the day to avoid problems with getting the romance scene.
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes:
                        • Tell Allard about the plot.
                        • Help Wilhelmina in assassinating Allard.
                        • Refuse to aid Wilhelmina in assassinating Allard.
                        • Kill Allard without Wilhelmina.
                      • If you go to Brant for a hint on how to find the second piece of evidence (after finding the first piece of evidence), make sure that when the pop-up appears where you can show him your current findings to cancel out of it first so you can still try to secure his aid later on.
                      • When showing Brant the evidence to secure his aid (after getting BOTH pieces of evidence), make sure to first show him the Bribery evidence first and then choose the option to show him more. Then show him the Murder Report. Make sure to choose the bottom option and /NOT/ to beg for his aid to successfully secure it when you try to escape the Noble Quarter later on.
                      • When giving the evidence to Wilhelmina, make sure to be able to give the Murder Report and /not just/ the Bribery evidence, as the latter will result to Wilhelmina getting arrested.
                      • This quest is canceled if you fail to provide the evidence to Wilhelmina in 3 days.
                      • If you acquire the best outcome, you can unlock a dialogue option for Hugo when offering him a job during his quest line "Off the Pilfered Path".

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Every Rose Has Its Thorn Quest Walkthrough

                      Gather the Evidence for Wilhelmina

                      Every Rose Has Its Thorn is a time-limited quest. You must find the evidence that proves the crimes of Allard and give it to Wilhelmina within 3 days. If you are looking for information on "how to unlock" this quest, you can find it listed above. After speaking to Wilhelmina, head back to Shakir's Inn and take the Marcher's set. You should have acquired this set during the main quest "Disa's Plot", you find this inside a chest found in The Guardhouse. The Marcher's set will help disguise yourself and you can easily walk around since the castle is a restricted area.

                      Proceed to Sven's Chambers and speak to Sven. When speaking to Sven, he may ask you for a task that will trigger one of his quests, "A Veil of Gossamer Clouds'. Listen and take the quest item from him, then speak to him again and he'll mention Allard. He will tell you that there is a man Ser Patrick who can help you. Proceed to his home at night, Patrick's Estate", and speak to him, You'll find him inside his house [See Dragon's Dogma 2 Map]. Ensure you listen to what he says since he will give a tip about finding the first evidence. He will also help you infiltrate Allard's home by distracting him. Choose "Agree", and the scene will transition to where you'll find yourself outside.

                      At this point, you may choose to end the quest early by speaking to Allard and informing him of the plot against him. Please note that ending the quest in this way will prevent you from romancing Wilhelmina as she will be arrested. The reward is also much lower for this outcome as he only gives you 3,000 gold. If you instead decide to continue helping Wilhelmina, continue to the next step.

                      FIRST EVIDENCE: Allard's Estate is just near Patrick's home, it's on the left side if you're looking at the map [See Dragon's Dogma 2 Map]. Wait for Allard to leave his home before you enter, go up the second floor, and immediately go inside the first room near the staircase. In this office room, inspect the left-hand side and you'll find an oval-shaped painting of a flower. Examine it to find the Murder Report.

                      Map Location for the Second Evidence.

                      SECOND EVIDENCE: To obtain a tip on finding the second evidence, head to The Stardrop Inn at night and speak to Brant. Speak to him and choose "Tell me of the Myrmecoleon." When a pop-up appears giving you the option of giving him the murder report, just cancel out of it without giving it to him. He will tell you that you may find evidence from one of the merchants who resides in the merchant quarter. This is optional as you may be able to find the evidence yourself by going to where it is. The second piece of evidence is located near Clovis's Barberie. You can go inside the house and pick up the Bribery Investigation Findings on the table.

                       

                      Give Wilhelmina the Evidence

                      Before handing over the evidence to Wilhelmina, you have the option to secure Brant's assistance, though it’s completely up to you. While gaining his aid can prevent being pursued by guards later, it might affect the romantic outcome depending on how you present the evidence.

                      If you decide to seek his aid, head back to The Stardrop Inn (at night), then choose "Tell me of the Myrmecoleon." Note that other players have had trouble with this so you may need to follow the exact order shown here. Show him the Bribery Investigation Findings first. After he reviews the evidence, choose the next dialogue, "Search for more evidence." Speak to him again and then show him the other piece of evidence. After he checks it, you'll get another dialogue option. Choose "I'll have any aid you can give." Choosing to beg for his aid will result in failing to receive it. This will update your quest log, showing you've secured the captain's aid.

                      Once you’ve decided whether to involve Brant, proceed to the Rose Chateau Bordelrie and speak with Wilhelmina in her room. If you obtained Brant's aid, he will have informed you that only the Murder Report is viable evidence to present, as the Bribery Investigation Findings aren't enough to sway the palace. If you didn’t involve Brant, you’ll still have the option to give Wilhelmina both pieces of evidence. It’s crucial to hand over both, or at least the Murder Report, as presenting only the Bribery Investigation Findings may result in her sudden arrest.

                       

                      Outcome 1: Help Wilhelmina in assassinating Allard

                      After giving her the bribery and murder report, Wilhelmina will ask if you intend to see this through to the end. Choose "Continue offering your aid," and Wilhelmina will change into a disguise. Follow her outside, and she'll tell you that Allard is inside the room next to hers. Go inside and tackle Allard to the ground to trigger a cutscene that reveals Wilhelmina's true identity and Allard's assassination.

                      After the cutscene, escape through the window by jumping into the hay below. If you previously, and successfully, chose to secure Brant's aid, a dialogue cutscene will trigger with him. He's not happy about your participation in killing Allard, but he will help you escape the premises safely. If you previously chose not to secure his aid, you will get a dialogue with Wilhelmina asking you to visit her later. Then the guards will begin chasing you. You can kill them, but if you'd rather avoid bloodshed you can run outside of town while pressing the To me! command at your pawns to prevent them from killing guards. After running a short distance outside of town, the music will return to normal. At this point, the guards will have forgotten you and you are free to enter the city again.

                      Regardless of how you escape, if you have a house in Vermund already, when you go home you'll find a note from Wilhelmina expressing her affection for you right by your door. Next, rest until night and then return to Wilhelmina. Speak to her and a romance cutscene will play out. This will also formally complete the quest and you'll be rewarded with 8,500 G, x1 Ring of Benevolence, and 1,200 XP.

                       

                      Outcome 2: Refuse to help Wilhelmina in assassinating Allard

                      After presenting the murder and bribery report to Wilhelmina, decline to help her when she asks if you intend to see this through till the end. She will gift you the Allheal Elixir at this time. This outcome will have Wilhelmina still murdering Allard, but you won't have the romance scene with her since the officials will capture her. Return to her room after a day to collect the remaining rewards from her attendant. The rewards for this outcome are worth more than the other two. Altogether you will get 1,200 XP, 7,500 G, 3 Tiger Eye and an Allheal Elixir.

                       

                      Outcome 3: Tell Allard of the Plot

                      After asking Patrick to summon Allard to his home, you can speak to Allard after he leaves his house and inform him of the plot against him. Please note that ending the quest in this way will prevent you from romancing Wilhelmina as she will be arrested. This outcome has the smallest reward with only 3,000 G and 1,200 XP.

                       

                      Outcome 4: Kill Allard without Wilhelmina

                      After handing Wilhelmina the bribery and murder report, she asks if you plan to see it through. By choosing "Continue offering your aid," she dons a disguise and instructs you to follow her. She reveals that Allard is in the next room, instead of tackling Allard, kill him immediately while Wilhelmina is outside. Wilhelmina then expresses dissatisfaction, lamenting her missed revenge. Before parting, she directs you to meet her at the Rose Chateau the next day. However, when you arrive, Rolf informs you that Wilhelmina has been implicated in Allard's murder and is no longer present.

                       

                      DD2 Every Rose Has Its Thorn Quest Objectives

                      • Gather evidence within three days.
                      • Deliver the evidence to Captain Brant to secure his aid.
                      • Deliver the evidence to Wilhelmina.
                      • Speak to Wilhelmina at night.

                       

                      Every Rose Has Its Thorn Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. Outcome 1: Helped Wilhelmina assassinate Allard

                      Nothing good can come of vengeance, and yet to deny it is to deny oneself the chance to move forward. With your aid, Wilhelmina saw her revenge wrought to satisfaction, and has pledged to aid you in turn.

                      Outcome 2: Refused to help Wilhelmina assassinate Allard

                      Nothing good can come of vengeance, and yet to deny it is to deny oneself the chance to move forward. Wilhelmina sought closure at any cost, and wrought her revenge in exchange for her own freedom.

                      Outcome 3: Tell Allard of the Plot

                      Believing that vengeance begets only suffering, you decided to inform Allard of the plot against his life. Fortune willing, this will prevent the tragedy from being repeated.

                      Helped Wilhelmina assassinate Allard - Every Rose Has Its Thorn Rewards:

                      • 1,200 XP
                      • 8,500 G
                      • 1 Ring of Benevolence
                      • Unlocks Romance Scene with Wilhelmina

                      Refused to help Wilhelmina assassinate Allard

                      • 1,200 XP
                      • 7,500 G
                      • 3 Tiger Eye
                      • 1 Allheal Elixir

                      Tell Allard of the plot

                      • 3,000 G
                      • 1,200 XP

                      Kill Allard without Wilhelmina

                      • 7,000 g
                      • 3 Tiger Eye
                      • 1,200 XP

                                                                      Since info on this page and the comments is conflicted, I'll offer my experience after just finishing this quest where I got both pieces of evidence, showed them to BOTH Brant and Wilhelmina, and got Wilhelmina's romance scene:

                                                                      1. Follow the initial quest requirements on this current page to get Wilhelmina in a state where she's locked in the side room and Rolf tells you she's busy with "a patron" (NOTE: I don't know if affinity matters because I gave her only one Bunch of Flowers when I first met her, but then left her in the "patron" room for literal weeks because I completely forgot about the peephole mechanic and didn't know how to progress the quest)
                                                                      2. Use the peephole to see her having a conversation with Allard, after which she'll talk to you in her room where you can offer your assistance to start the quest
                                                                      3. You now have three days to finish the quest, so hop over to Sven's room and talk to him to learn about Patrick (NOTE: if you have multiple active quests involving Sven, you may need to talk to him multiple times to get the correct dialogue)
                                                                      4. Pass time till night, go to Patrick's Estate, then agree to having him distract Allard
                                                                      5. Head over to Allard's house, you'll probably see him walking out the front door by the time you get there, just wait a minute for him to pass then head inside
                                                                      6. Go to the second floor room specified in the current guide to inspect the small painting above a small table to get the Murder Report
                                                                      7. Now go to the Vernworth portcrystal and refer to the circled building on the current guide to pick up the Bribery Investigation Report, which is just sitting on a table inside
                                                                      8. Head over to the tavern and talk to Brant, ask him about the Myrmecoleon, show him the Bribery Investigation Findings, then back out of any other dialogue options (NOTE: do NOT ask for his aid yet)
                                                                      9. Talk to Brant again, ask him about the Myrmecoleon again, show him the Murder Report, then choose "I'll have any aid you can give" (NOTE: do NOT "beg")
                                                                      10. Check your inventory, you should still have BOTH pieces of evidence (Brant does NOT keep either document, but you also do NOT receive the usual notification for offering something to an NPC and getting it back)
                                                                      11. Pass time until morning, then head over to the Rose Chateau Borderlie, talk to Wilhelmina, and offer her both pieces of evidence (NOTE: offering just ONE piece of evidence will cause the menu to automatically select the proceed/confirm option, make sure you navigate to the other document slot and offer BOTH of them before proceeding)
                                                                      12. Wilhelmina gives an oddly short affirmation of the evidence then talks about confronting Allard, choose to "continue offering your aid"
                                                                      13. Wilhelmina will put on a disguise and tell you to go into the room to subdue Allard, run in and tackle him to proceed to the cutscene
                                                                      14. After the cutscene where she kills Allard, a guard will knock on the door and Wilhelmina will tell you to jump out the window to escape
                                                                      15. Immediately escaping out the window should automatically initiate a dialogue with Brant outside where he starts to question your actions, but acknowledges that making an escape is a more pressing matter. Follow him out of the Noble's Quarter and he should talk to you again near the portcrystal
                                                                      16. Brant will express shock at your decision to kill Allard, but will say that he'll still do everything he can to obfuscate your involvement before noting that you may still have to deal with the consequences of your actions when the time comes (NOTE: I do not know what consequences, if any, this will cause)
                                                                      17. Pass time for at least one day then go talk to Wilhelmina and you should automatically get her romance cutscene upon entering her room, as well as the associated rewards after the cutscene

                                                                      After following these steps, Allard is dead, Brant is a bit upset, and Wilhelmina has both gotten her revenge and been successfully romanced. After the quest completes Wilhelmina no longer seems to be inside the Borderlie, even after a full day's rest, but that's an investigation for another day

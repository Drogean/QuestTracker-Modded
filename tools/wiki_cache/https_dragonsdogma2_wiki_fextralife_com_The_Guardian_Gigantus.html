                      The Guardian Gigantus | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      The Guardian Gigantus is a Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). This quest has you battling Talos, The Guardian Gigantus, and preventing the giant from reaching The Moonglint Tower. This has not only you and your pawn battling the giant, but also has Soldier NPCs assisting in stopping Gigantus. This quest is started automatically as players complete the quest A New Godsway. This quest is affected by the passage of time. However, this quest will play out regardless of the Player's actions, which will influence rewards, cutscenes and later content.

                       

                      General Info

                      • Next: Legacy
                      • Location: Flamebearer Palace Spellseal Door
                      • Bosses: Talos

                      The Guardian Gigantus Dragon's Dogma 2 Quest Details

                      How to Unlock The Guardian Gigantus in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This quest unlocks automatically after completing the quest, A New Godsway. [See Dragon's Dogma 2 Map]

                      The Guardian Gigantus Quest Description

                      The Guardian Gigantus - Quest Start Description

                      Ambrosius has instructed you to pass the Godsbane blade he crafted from a relic of the Forgotten Sovran into the hands of Lord Phaesus.

                      The Guardian Gigantus - Quest End Description

                      Talos, the stone gigantus, has emerged from the sea and begun to march for Volcanic Island, leaving chaos in its wake. Though Phaesus witnesses this destruction, he does not break stride, leaving you little choice but to follow him to Moonglint Tower if you mean to uncover his plans.

                      The Guardian Gigantus Quest Objectives

                      • Take the Godsbane to Phaesus
                      • You encountered the gigantus.

                       

                      The Guardian Gigantus Dragon's Dogma 2 Quest Walkthrough

                      Take the Godsbane to Phaesus

                      After obtaining the Empowered Godsbane Blade from Ambrosius, exit the Forbidden Magick Research Lab and head toward Phaesus. Upon leaving, a cutscene reveals Phaesus and his men unlocking and entering the Flamebearer Palace Spellseal Door. Next, proceed south of Bakbattahl and use the Empowered Godsbane Blade to unlock the doors. If you delay this quest, the scene will eventually play out regardless of your presence.

                      Upon reaching the Flamebearer Palace Spellseal Door, interact with it, prompting your character to lift the blade and unlock the entrance. Follow the rocky path until nearing a cliffside, where a cutscene reveals Talos, the Guardian Gigantus, emerging from the ocean and heading toward Volcanic Island. After the cutscene, you can freely explore again, seeing Talos approaching the island. If the quest is ignored, you may still hear the distant footsteps of the Guardian Gigantus.

                      You’ll need to stay on the path ahead to intercept the monster before it reaches Moonglint Tower. Use any means necessary to slow down Talos. Continue following the quest marker to keep up with the Guardian Gigantus. Upon arriving on the island surrounded by water, a cutscene will show the Guardian Gigantus fixating on Phaesus and his men.

                       

                      Slowing Down Guardian Gigantus

                      As Gigantus makes his slow advance toward Moonglint Tower, you’ll need to find a way to stop him. Fortunately, his pace gives you time to act. Target the glowing spikes on his body, these are his weak points. Melee strikes can break off the spikes, causing a bleeding effect, while ranged attacks are effective from the water. If he reaches land, use the high ground to jump onto him and access weak spots, or employ the large artillery bows manned by soldiers defending the tower. 

                      You'll need to capitalize on climbing different points of the body, and make sure that there are pawns on the ground so that if you fall, they can try to catch you. As Gigantus progresses towards the tower, Soldier NPCs will slowly grow in number to try and stop him, this will cause Gigantus to be stunned by the reign of fire that it is receiving, this will give you some time to attack his remaining weak spots. If you manage to knock out all the weak spots, you can start attacking it until Gigantus dies before he reaches Moonglint Tower. Also, if you fail to bring down Gigantus, he will eventually be defeated by the NPC Soldiers who are also trying to stop it just before he gets to the base of the tower. Letting the NPCs handle Gigantus will just refrain you from getting any reward, but if you do actually defeat Gigantus, you will be rewarded with 19,000 XP.

                      Once done, head over to the Moonglint Tower Entrance and a cutscene will play out. It's important to take note that even if you decide to not battle Gigantus, you'll still be able to go to Moonglint Tower and this quest will eventually be logged as completed.

                      The Guardian Gigantus Dragon's Dogma 2 Trivia, Notes and Tips

                      • This quest unlocks the related quest: Legacy
                      • Unlocking this quest requires the player to complete: A New Godsway
                      • This quest has no multiple outcomes.
                      • Gigantus, I Hardly Knew Ye: To get this trophy, you must break all the weak spots before it gets to the cutscene where it gets shot with a ballista and loses its arm. 
                      • Back Where it all Began: This is a story-related trophy, and can't be missed. During The Guardian Gigantus main quest, you will reach the area in which the game started. As soon as you are there, the trophy will unlock.
                      • Stopping Talos (Guardian Gigantus) by using a Preserved Medusa Head or Medusa Head item will potentially lock you out of When Wills Collide quest. Therefore, it is recommended that you stop Talos by destroying his weak spots.
                      • Depending on how quickly the Player stops Talos by destroying his weak spots, the less damage the entire structure takes and the above mentioned quest will play out differently. Talos may lose both, one, or no arms. When both arms are intact, which is a requirement for the aforementioned achievement, Talos will be able to function optimally during "When Wills Collide". One arm leads to a performance at half capacity and if Talos is fully destroyed (eg. by ignoring "The Guardian Gigantus" quest), the Player will be locked out of "When Wills Collide" for that playthrough.

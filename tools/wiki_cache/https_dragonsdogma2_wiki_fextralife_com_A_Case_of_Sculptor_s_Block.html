                      A Case of Sculptor's Block
                      Quest Location Klark's Estate (Noble Quarter)
                      NPC Pickup Klark
                      Requirements Progress the game and approach Klark
                      Time Limited No
                      Quest Canceled n/a
                      Unlocks n/a
                      Other NPCs Fulvio
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes
                      Rewards The sculpture is perfect
                      4500 XP
                      18500 G

                      The sculpture was carved poorly
                      4500 XP
                      7000 G

                      A Case of Sculptor's Block is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). A Case of Sculptor's Block is not affected by the passage of time. A man named Klark asks for the Arisen's help, he requests a special sculpture piece he would like to add to his home.

                       

                      You happened upon the estate of a noble marked for its collection of sculptures, but it seems to be one masterpiece short. Perhaps a word with the sculptor himself is in order.

                       

                      How to Unlock A Case of Sculptor's Block Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      Players can get this quest early in the game, simply progress the story and approach Klark at the Noble Quarter area of Vernworth. Klark can be found standing outside his house, "Klark's Estate". See A Case of Sculptor's Block on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 A Case of Sculptor's Block Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest has multiple outcomes:
                        • The battle against the Griffin was long enough and Fulvio was able to capture all the details of the Griffin.
                        • The battle against the Griffin had only ample time and Fulvio only captured some details of the Griffin.
                        • The battle against the Griffin was too short and Fulvio failed to capture the details of the Griffin.
                      • Progress the story to unlock this quest and approach Klark at the Noble Quarter in Vernworth.
                      • We recommend resting at an inn before meeting Fulvio at the Griffin's Nest so you can reload your save file if you're not happy with the end result of the sculpture or if you accidentally did something wrong.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 A Case of Sculptor's Block Quest Walkthrough

                      Visit Fulvio in Battahl

                      This quest is not affected by the passage of time or "time-limited", and progressing this quest requires the player to reach Bakbattahl located in the region of Battahl. Players can only reach this area after completing the quest "Feast of Deception", where you'll receive the border entry permit from Brant. When you arrive at Bakbattahl, head to Fulvio's Workshop to progress the quest.

                      Head inside the workshop and speak to Fulvio. You'll learn from him that he is having trouble devising ideas to create the "perfect" sculpture and suggests that you accompany him in finding and fighting a Griffin. The only gist is, that you'll need to somehow "prolong" the battle for as long as possible so that he can complete his sketch. Now there are two ways to approach this

                      1. Obtain the "Medusa Head" or a "Preserved Medusa Head" and use it at the Griffin to turn it into stone.
                      2. Equip low level/stats weapons to minimize the damage inflicted on the beast and to prevent it from flying away.
                      3. Put the Griffin to Sleep and wait until Fulvio advises you that it's done

                       

                      How to Get Medusa's Head

                      Medusa can be located in the Caliginous Depths [See Dragon's Dogma 2 Map], and decapitating her will result in acquiring two versions of the body part, depending on what you did when fighting the Gorgon. Getting the "Medusa Head" will also suffice but it will eventually decay if you don't immediately rush to the location where the Griffin is. Players will get the regular Medusa Head if they inflict a lot of damage to her health and eventually, cut off her head. On the other hand, if you immediately cut off her head while keeping her well-being in a healthy state, you can obtain the "Preserved Medusa Head". But again, either version of Medusa's head will still work against the Griffin.

                      • EASY TIP TO GET A PRESERVED MEDUSA HEAD:
                        • STEP 1: Requires the Warfarer vocation
                        • STEP 2: Equip weapons for Magick Archer and Warrior
                        • STEP 3: When you enter the creature's den, use the skill Soporific Bolt
                        • STEP 4: Once Medusa is put to sleep, quickly approach her head and swap to a Warrior weapon.
                        • STEP 5: Use the skill Indomitable Lash to cut off the creature's head.
                        • ALTERNATE TIP: Using a thief weapon will also work and repetitive slices will eventually cut off her head.

                       

                      Meet Fulvio at the Griffin's Nest

                      NOTE: We recommend resting at an inn before meeting Fulvio so you can reload your save file if you're not happy with the end result of the sculpture or if you accidentally did something wrong.

                      The location of the Griffin's nest will be pinned to your map, it's located northwest of Bakbattahl. When you arrive, you'll find Fulvio waiting for you by the entrance. Speak to him and he'll lead you to the location of the Griffin. Now again, the goal here is to prolong the battle for as long as possible, and make sure that Fulvio does not die here! This NPC can die since he will be around the Griffin's nest trying to complete his sketch.

                      • Before approaching the Griffin, make sure to tell your pawns to WAIT. If the Griffin is turned into stone, this will prevent them from attacking and breaking the petrified beast into pieces.
                      • The same goes if you want to put the beast to sleep (such as Magick Archer's Soporific Bolt), command your pawns to WAIT. So that they will avoid attacking the Griffin while it is asleep.
                      • If you go in to fight the beast, as mentioned, make sure to prolong the battle for as long as possible. And once the Griffin flies away, Fulvio will mention that his sketch is done.
                      • If Fulvio captured all the details, he would say something like "'Tis done! With this sketch, I will doubtless be able to chisel a fine griffin!"
                      • If the time wasn't enough, he would say something like "I suppose that will have to do. I'll have to use my imagination for the finer details,"

                      Whichever way how you approach the Griffin, after Fulvio completes his sketch, wait a few days and visit his workshop in Bakbattahl and he'll tell you to meet him at Klark's Estate in Vernworth to unveil the sculpture. Back at Klark's Estate, before heading to the second floor, take this time to search around the house to find loot. When you're ready, proceed to the second floor and head inside the room on the left to trigger a cutscene where you'll see the sculpture.

                       

                      DD2 A Case of Sculptor's Block Quest Objectives

                      • Visit Fulvio in Battahl
                      • Accompany Fulvio.
                      • Wait a few days, then check on Fulvio's progress.
                      • Attend the unveiling at Klark's estate.

                       

                      A Case of Sculptor's Block Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. Outcome 1: Fulvio had enough time to capture every detail of the Griffin.

                      A product of your daring and his own talent, Fulvio's sculpture is breathtakingly lifelike, capturing every detail of the griffin's petrified form. Hailed as a masterpiece and greeted with thunderous applause, this proud achievement will see the sculptor's reputation soar higher than griffin wing ever could.

                      Outcome 2: Fulvio only had ample time to capture the details of the Griffin

                      Having had ample opportunity to observe the griffin during your battle, Fulvio was able to render it faithfully in stone. The sculpture's unveiling was met with enthusiastic applause, and the promising future of its creator is all but assured.

                      Outcome 3: The battle against the Griffin was too short and Fulvio failed to capture the details he needed.

                      Though you fought valiantly against the griffin, the battle was too short-lived for Fulvio to capture the beast in its full glory. The result was a sculpture as rough as the sketch on which it was based, and the tarnished reputation of one promising young sculptor.

                      The Griffin is sculpted to perfection and the guests are highly impressed. - A Case of Sculptor's Block Rewards:

                      • 4500 XP
                      • 0 Dcp
                      • 18500 G

                      The Griffin Sculpture was carved poorly and the guests laughed. - A Case of Sculptor's Block Rewards:

                      • 4500 XP
                      • 0 Dcp
                      • 7000 G

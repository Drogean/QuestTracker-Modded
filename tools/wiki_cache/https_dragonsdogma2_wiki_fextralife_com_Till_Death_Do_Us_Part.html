                      Till Death Do Us Part | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      Till Death Do Us Part
                      Quest Location Watchhead's Home, Vernworth
                      NPC Pickup Margit
                      Requirements Hear the rumor about Ser Ludolph from Oswald and speak to Margit
                      Time Limited Yes
                      Quest Canceled You fail to rescue Gregor
                      Unlocks None
                      Other NPCs Oswald, Gregor
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes
                      Rewards You rescued Ser Gregor and got him home before the runner:
                      2800 XP
                      0 Dcp
                      5000 G
                      x1 Steeled Virtue
                      x1 Lustrous Targe

                      You failed to rescue Ser Gregor:
                      2800 XP
                      0 Dcp
                      5000G
                      x1 Steeled Virtue

                      Till Death Do Us Part is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). Till Death Do Us Part is affected by the passage of time. This quest can be picked up by speaking to Margit, the wife of Gregor, after you've heard of rumors that have been told of Ser Ludolph. Margit asks for your help to rescue her husband before it's too late.

                       

                      The lecherous Ser Ludolph has no qualms about abusing his station to get what he wants. And when that happens to be Gregor's wife, he has the man sent on an impossible mission.

                       

                      How to Unlock Till Death Do Us Part Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      To unlock this quest, players must first hear the rumor about Ser Ludolph from an NPC named Oswald. He is located next to a fountain at the Noble Quarter of Vernworth, near the Townsend's Estate. Once you know of the rumor, speak to Margit who can be found outside the Watchhead's Home in Vernworth. See Till Death Do Us Part on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Till Death Do Us Part Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This is a Time-Sensitive quest.
                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest
                      • This quest has multiple multiple outcomes:
                        • You rescued Gregor.
                        • You failed to rescue Gregor.
                      • This quest requires you to complete The Nameless Village quest and to learn about the rumor about Ser Ludolph before speaking to Margit
                      • This quest is canceled or fails if you ignore the passage of time and Gregor dies.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Till Death Do Us Part Quest Walkthrough

                      Speak to Margit

                      To start, this quest is influenced by the passage of time, making it essential to complete it quickly to assist Gregor. The primary objective is to help him survive, as achieving this will yield the best rewards from the quest. Make it a priority to reach him on time to prevent his death.

                       

                      Click on the images above to enlarge them.

                      Before you can start this quest, you must first hear the rumor about Ser Ludolph's interest in Margit, even though she is married to Gregor. In our experience, we learned this rumor from Oswald after completing The Nameless Village quest. If you've advanced far enough in the main story, be sure to talk to Oswald about it before approaching Margit, who can be found outside the Watchhead's Home in Vernworth. See Till Death Do Us Part on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map]

                      When you approach her, a dialogue option will appear. Select "Ask about Ser Ludolph", and she will inform you about Gregor's location, which is at the Illdoers' Resting Place [See Dragon's Dogma 2 Map]. Having heard the rumors, you'll have an additional dialogue option to choose from, so select "Tell her of Ser Ludolph's plot". She will then request that you save Gregor. Be sure to accept her request to officially trigger the quest and begin your mission.

                       

                      Outcome 1: Rescued Gregor

                      The ideal outcome of this quest is to arrive in time and prevent Gregor's death. After your conversation with Margit, your next goal is to head to the Illdoers' Resting Place in the Misty Marshes, located northwest of Vernworth. It's advisable to carry a camping kit, as you'll find a campsite about halfway to your destination. Upon your arrival, you'll witness Gregor battling a Dullahan, a legendary figure from Irish folklore typically represented as a headless horseman.

                      When you arrive, be ready to aid Gregor and his men. Take note that the Dullahan is one of the most powerful enemies you'll face, so prepare all your recovery potions, items that can buff the team, and Wakestones. In this encounter, you're not supposed to defeat the Dullahan, but instead, just survive, aid your team, make sure Gregor does not die, and apply enough damage to make the creature flee. Once the area is safe, speak to Gregor, and report back to Margit. This will complete the quest and you'll receive the following rewards for saving Gregor: 2800 XP, 5000 G, x1 Steeled Virtue, and x1 Lustrous Targe.

                       

                      Outcome 2: Failed to Rescue Gregor

                      For this outcome, it's somehow obvious that you failed to rescue Gregor from the Dullahan for some reasons:

                      • He died during the fight with Dullahan.
                      • You did not reach him in time.
                      • You did not prioritize this quest and ignored the passage of time.
                      • One possible outcome before you pick up the quest: Gregor's already dead. He can die early on at the start of the game during the quest "In Dragon's Wake" when you're accompanying him to Vernworth since you will be encountering many creatures as the unit travels on foot.

                      If Gregor dies during combat, the quest will be updated to report this news to Margit. Upon talking to Margit, she will express shock and grief over the passing of her husband. She will not attribute any fault to you for his death. This completes the quest, and you will receive all quest rewards as if he would had survived except for the Lustrous Targe.

                       

                      DD2 Till Death Do Us Part Quest Objectives

                      • Speak to Margit
                      • Aid Gregor at the Illdoers' Resting Place.
                      • Report back to Margit.

                       

                      Till Death Do Us Part Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. Outcome 1: You rescued Gregor.

                      Gregor returned to his wife none the worse for wear, foiling Ser Ludolph's latest scheme. All's well that ends well, but it seems prudent to tread carefully around the nobility in future.

                      Outcome 2: You failed to rescue Gregor.

                      Gregor fell prey to Ser Ludolph's horrid scheme, leaving his lady wife a widow. But poor Margit's troubles are far from done, as the lecherous nobleman has no intention of leaving her alone, and her prospects of fending off his underhanded tactics are surely as slim as her late husband's.

                      You rescued Gregor. - Till Death Do Us Part Rewards:

                      • 2800 XP
                      • 0 Dcp
                      • 5000 G
                      • x1 Steeled Virtue
                      • x1 Lustrous Targe

                      You failed to rescue Gregor. - Till Death Do Us Part Rewards:

                      • 2800 XP
                      • 0 Dcp
                      • 5000 G
                      • x1 Steeled Virtue

                                                                                        You do not need to do the Nameless Village quest or even trigger enough main story (Brant's questline) to unlock it for availability. Just speak to the fat nobleman standing by the fountain in the midst of the Noble Quarter in Vernworth.

                                                                                        Directions: on the map, the Comptroller's House is to the left of Watch Head's house (Gregor and Margit's house). Across the street from those houses is a stairway and the Townsend's Estate (that estate is directly opposite Comp). In front of the estate, to its left on the map, is the fountain. Find that fat guy there.

                      Spellbound is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). Spellbound is affected by the passage of time (during the scenario where Trysha casts her spells). You will meet a young girl named Trysha, she's curious about learning magick and will ask the Arisen for help to procure 5 grimoires. Interestingly, Myrddin also seeks the very same tomes that she's looking for.

                       

                      You encountered Trysha, a young girl living with her grandparents on the outskirts of Vermund. Fed up with her dreary days, she dreams of only one thing: studying magick.

                       

                      How to Unlock Spellbound Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This quest can be started by reaching Eini's Home. After meeting Trysha and Eini, the quest Spellbound will start. (Eini and Eino must be out of the house for Trysha to talk to you about the quest. You can pick them up and remove them from the house if you need to.) See Spellbound on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Spellbound Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest but is related to The Sorcerer's Appraisal, a quest where another man named Myrddin is also looking for the same grimoires as Trysha.
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes:
                        • You failed to help Trysha control her magick
                        • You gave Trysha the copies
                        • You helped Trysha control her magick
                      • This quest has no prerequisites
                      • This quest is canceled if you don't pick it up before ??
                      • By completing Spellbound and increasing their affinity with Trysha, players receive Conjurer's Jottings which can be used to unlock the Meteoron skill. This counts towards the Master of the Maisters gold trophy

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Spellbound Quest Walkthrough

                      Gather the Grimoires

                      The first part of this quest involves procuring 3-5 Grimoires that Trysha is looking for. This guide will indicate their locations and how to retrieve each tome. NOTE: Two NPCs seek the same tomes and for 2 different quests. The other quest is The Sorcerer's Appraisal, where you must collect these books for Myrddin. Once you have collected enough tomes, it is possible to forge them by using Ibrahim's forgery service in Checkpoint Rest Town so you can decide if you want to give the originals to Trysha or Myrddin, or if you want to look for more original tomes to satisfy both quests.

                      This quest is best completed before The Sorcerer's Appraisal, since this will positively affect the latter.

                      We recommend giving 3 originals to Trysha as you will otherwise fail to obtain the maister skill. By giving her the real books instead of her father, you will receive 2 maister skills upon completing Spellbound. While there is an option to give her all 5 tomes, it is not necessary to get all of the quest rewards and doesn't seem to carry any additional benefits. This should leave you with 4 more potential original tomes for the other sorcerer Maister quest and save you some gold on forging.

                      Find Trysha at Eini's Home due North of Melve near the top of the map. To accept or progress her quest, speak to her when her grandparents are away, since they have forbidden her to study magic. If they won't leave the house, pick up Trysha (R2) and set her outside and then talk to her. Give her 3 of the 5 Grimoires she seeks at one time to gain the Turquoise Ring. Then wait a few days, speak to Trysha again, and give her the rest of the tomes (optional, you may tell her that the 3 tomes you have delivered are the last ones). The locations of the tomes can be found below, including Ibrahim's Shop. Make sure both grandparents are alive when on the step to check on Trysha studies. Eini can sometimes die, which was prevents her magic fenzy event. After reviving Eini back at Vernworth with a wakestone, you can progress the quest and get the Meteoron skill.

                      Let There Be Light: Located in Myrddin's Home, found on the desk upstairs. When stealing this from Myrddin, go through the balcony from the outside but not up the stairs from the inside. If he catches you, he will attack you on the spot. 

                      Fulminous Shield: Purchased from Dudley in Melve [DD2 Map here]

                      Nation's Death Knell: Found in Ancient Battleground, located northeast of the Checkpoint Rest Town. The tome is found inside a chest located next to a cenotaph within the castle ruins (still part of the dungeon)

                      This book is also lootable in Waterfall Cave to the East of Borderwatch Outpost. The book is in a chest on the top floor of the cavern, and will spawn a Lich boss when looted. Warning: In each playthrough there are only two original Nation's Death Knell. If you already gave both of them to Trysha and Myrdinn, the quest Tolled to Rest cannot be finished, which will also influence the outcome of Off the Pilfered Path. Therefore, it's better to finish Tolled to Rest before giving Nation's Death Knell to them.

                      Howling Blizzard: Wendy from the Nameless Village wants to exchange the Howling Blizzard for a Fulminous Shield. However, you don't have to give her the original, because the collection will be incomplete and will force you to abandon the quest. Instead, ask Ibrahim in Checkpoint Rest Town to forge the Fulminous Shield and give it to Wendy

                      This book is also lootable in Waterfall Cave to the East of Borderwatch Outpost. The book is in a chest on the top floor of the cavern, and will spawn a Lich boss when looted.

                      Towering Earth: This can be acquired by speaking with Waldhar after he moves to The Gracious Hand's Vaults (see The Caged Magistrate and The Heel of History)

                       

                       

                      Outcome 1: Deliver the Grimoires

                      NOTE: Ibrahim is an NPC found in Checkpoint Rest Town. He can do forgeries for a fee, which is a copy of the original, but will not produce the same results as the original. Two NPCs seek the same tomes and for 2 different quests. The other quest is The Sorcerer's Appraisal, where you must collect these books for Myrddin. Your choice will affect the results of this quest and you may want to get copies of the Grimoires so you can give the copies to Myrddin should you have a hard time locating other original tomes, and the originals to Trysha.

                      After giving her 3-5 Grimoires, return to Eini's Home and you will find Trysha conjuring spells and she ends up attacking those close to her. NOTE: Do not attack Trysha because you will not receive the rewards that you're aiming for. You can either avoid the spells or maintain your distance. Wait for her spells to finish and then get near Trysha, and press RT to tackle her. You will see this window of opportunity when she stops to recover her stamina and that's when you can get near her. Alternatively, being a mage yourself allows you to use the "High Solemnity" or "Solemnity" spell on Trysha, thus stopping her from using spells further and allows you tackle her more easily and immediately. Alternatively, it is possible to kill Trysha and use a Wakestone on her body to bring her back to life. Return to Eini's Home again after a few days to check how Trysha is doing. This time the quest Spellbound will be marked as completed and she will give you the Conjurer's Jottings while Eini will give the Enchanter's Almanac.

                      If you have an issue where they aren't giving you the skill scrolls, you may need to increase their affinity. You can do this by giving them gifts (once per day) or completing escort quests for them. Once their affinity level is high enough, they will give you the skill scroll upon next speaking to them.

                       

                      Outcome 2: You failed to help Trysha control her magick

                      This outcome happens in the instance that you fail to control Trysha after giving her the Grimoires. When you return to visit her at Eini's Home a few days after giving her the tomes, she unleashes her magick and is uncontrollable. Attacking and not tackling her at the right time will cause this outcome. 

                       

                      Outcome 3: Deliver the forged tomes to Trysha

                      The Walkthrough for this outcome is a work in progress. If the details are available, please feel free to add them here.

                       

                      DD2 Spellbound Quest Objectives

                      • Gather the Grimoires
                      • Bring Trysha the Grimoires
                      • Visit Trysha after a few days
                      • Exhaust Trysha's magick reserves
                      • Check on Trysha after a few days

                       

                      Spellbound Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. You gave Trysha the forged tomes:

                      Living in the far-flung outskirts of Vermund with only her grandparents for company, it is hardly any wonder that Trysha came to be so fascinated with magick. Alas, the counterfeit grimoires you gave her aren't likely to see the specter of boredom banished anytime soon.

                      You gave Trysha forged books - Spellbound Reward:

                      • 2000 XP
                      • 3000 G

                      You failed to help Trysha control her magick:

                      Living in the far-flung outskirts of Vermund with only her grandparents for company, it is hardly any wonder that Trysha came to be so fascinated with magick. Alas, it seems her wish was doomed from the outset, as no sooner had her studies begun than her magick spiraled out of control. Unable to help her, you took her life instead.

                      You helped Trysha control her magick:

                      Living in the far-flung outskirts of Vermund with only her grandparents for company, it is hardly any wonder that Trysha came to be so fascinated with magick. But it seems her studies were forbidden with good reason, for no sooner had she begun to dabble than her magick spiraled out of control. Fortunately, however, you managed to exhaust her arcane reserves and stem the magickal deluge before it claimed her life.

                      You helped Trysha control her magick - Spellbound Rewards:

                      • 2,000 XP
                      • 3,000 G
                      • Cast Stone
                      • Conjurer's Jottings
                      • Enchanter's Almanac

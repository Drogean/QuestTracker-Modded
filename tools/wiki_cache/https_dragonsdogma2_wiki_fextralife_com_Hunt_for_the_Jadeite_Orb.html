                      Hunt for the Jadeite Orb is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). Hunt for the Jadeite Orb is not affected by the passage of time and takes place in Checkpoint Rest Town where two men seek the same treasure- the Jadeite Orb. This quest will let you decide who you want to help and how. 

                       

                      On your wanderings, you encountered a man desperate to reclaim a stolen treasure: the Jadeite Orb.

                       

                      How to Unlock Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      You will unlock Hunt for the Jadeite Orb by speaking to Everard or Offulve at the Checkpoint Rest Town. You will find Everard outside Celeste's Smithy, while Offulve is at the front of The Sword and Staff Inn where the people are gathered. See Hunt for the Jadeite Orb on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest.
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes:
                        • Give the Jadeite Orb to Everard
                        • Give the Jadeite Orb to Offulve
                        • Give each of them a Jadeite Orb (the Sphinx can duplicate the Jadeite Orb during the Riddle of Conviction in A Game of Wits if one does not wish to use this opportunity for a different item, such as a Portcrystal)
                        • Deceive Everard by giving him a forgery
                        • Deceive Offulve by giving him a forgery
                      • This quest has no prerequisites.
                      • This quest is canceled if you don't pick it up before ??

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Quest Walkthrough

                      Find the Jadeite Orb

                      NOTE: Two NPCs will ask for the same item. This quest can be picked up either by talking to Offulve or Everard, it doesn't matter who you talk to first, but you need to talk to both of them so this quest can be marked as completed later on.

                      As you explore Checkpoint Rest Town, you will find an NPC with the name Everard. You will find him standing outside Celeste's Smithy and he immediately recognizes you as the Arisen. At first, he will ask you if you have encountered a man named Offulve and will ask if you have seen a Jadeite Orb. He candidly expresses that locating the treasure is far more important than the man. He promises that you will be rewarded handsomely for your help.

                      To progress this quest, look for Ibrahim's Scrap Store. This store is located behind Everard's location behind a thick bush. It's a little difficult to spot but keep going in this direction until you see the store neatly tucked in this small section of the town. Ibrahim is a resourceful man who deals with scraps and treasures. He can even forge items upon request and is in possession of a Jadeite Orb which he sells for 7,500 G. Purchase the Jadeite Orb from him and talk to Everard or Offulve, depending on who you want to give the Jadeite Orb to.

                       

                      Outcome 1: Give a genuine Jadeite Orb to Offulve and Everard

                      After purchasing the genuine Jadeite Orb from Ibrahim's Scrap Store, you need to progress A Game of Wits by finding the Sphinx's first location at the Mountain Shrine. During The Riddle of Conviction, the Sphinx can duplicate ANY item for you. We recommend giving her the Jadeite Orb so you can get 2 and give each to Offulve and Everard.

                      To start A Game of Wits, you first need to locate the Sphinx. Ideally, you will travel from Checkpoint Rest Town, found on the western side of the map. Then, follow the outlined path in the image below. You will travel to the Northern area and pass through the Ancient Battleground, an open area below the first entry point, Worldsend Cavern. A large portion of this area is a castle rubble that will lead to the entrance of the Worldsend Cavern. From there, follow the newly revealed path that will take you to the Shrine Corridor. You will find the entrance to the Mountain Shrine directly on top by the other side using the stairs. The legendary Sphinx is waiting for you at the top.

                      After receiving the Jadeite Orb from the Sphinx, you can return to Checkpoint Rest Town and give Everard and Offulve their genuine treasures. Everard will verify the authenticity of the item and take it to Ibrahim. Wait for him to come back and then receive your rewards from him. Then, look for Offulve near the Oxcart Station in front of The Sword & Staff Inn, then give him the genuine Jadeite Orb. Each of them will give you their rewards and doing this will mark the quest Hunt for the Jadeite Orb as completed. 

                       

                      Outcome 2: Deliver the Jadeite Orb to Everard

                      NOTE: Delivering the item to Everard will not complete the quest, so you still need to speak with Offulve and decide if you will be honest with him, or give him a forgery.  This quest has multiple outcomes and this walkthrough will guide players to complete the quest by giving Everard the genuine Jadeite Orb.

                      You will find Everard in the same spot where you first found him. Before you learn of his promised reward, Everard wants to verify the authenticity of the Jadeite Orb. He said that he trusts Ibrahim's eye most of all and wants to visit him. (If you have followed this walkthrough, simply return to where you got the item from).

                      As Ibrahim examines the Jadeite Orb that he just sold to you, Everard takes his leave and asks you to await his return. He will return after a minute and Everard will be pleased to know that you have given him the genuine article. This quest will not be marked as completed immediately because there is another man who asks for the same item.

                       

                      Offulve is the other NPC looking for the Jadeite Orb and can be found outside The Sword and Staff Inn where the other people are gathered. You will get another objective here to provide a second Jadeite Orb for Offulve. During our playthrough, we decided to not give him a forgery and instead, be honest with him. Talk to him again and you will have 3 options to respond: 

                      • Aye (Give the genuine article)
                      • Not as yet.
                      • 'Twas nowhere to be found.

                      Choosing the 3rd option will prompt Offulve to leave town and will mark the quest Hunt for the Jadeite Orb as completed and you will not receive any additional rewards from Offulve.

                       

                      Outcome 3: Deliver the Jadeite Orb to Offulve

                      NOTE: This quest has multiple outcomes and this walkthrough will guide players to complete the quest by giving Offulve the Jadeite Orb.

                      Giving the Imitation Jadeite Orb to Offulve will give you 1000 XP, an Elite Camping Kit, and 3,000 G. It seems that he was happy to take the Jadeite Orb immediately without verifying it. If you return to the Checkpoint Rest Town after completing the quest and gave Offulve a forgery, you can find him wandering the town. He will tell you that the Jadeite Orb you gave him turned out to be a forgery. He does not blame you for only finding a forgery but promises revenge if you deceived him on purpose.

                      The walkthrough and rewards for giving the genuine Jadeite Orb to Offulve is a work in progress. If the details are available, please feel free to add them here.

                        

                      Outcome 4: Give Everard a fake Jadeite Orb

                      The Walkthrough for this outcome is a work in progress. If the details are available, please feel free to add them here. 

                      NOTE: This quest has multiple outcomes and this walkthrough will guide players to complete the quest by giving Everard the Imitation Jadeite Orb. 

                      You can get the Imitation Jadeite Orb from Ibrahim by asking him to forge it, and then hand it over to Everard. If you do so, he will verify the authenticity of the item by again, going to Ibrahim. Everard will leave the store to do something while Ibrahim checks it, and you can bribe Ibrahim for a total of 12,000 G to lie for you. Alternatively, if you don't bribe Ibrahim fully, Everard will call the guards on you and you'll be forced to fight them off and potentially kill other NPCs who can give you quests. NOTE: If you attempt to flee before Everard returns, the screen will eventually fade to black and teleport Everard back to Ibrahim. He will call the guards and fight you.

                       

                      DD2 Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Quest Objectives

                      • Hunt for the Jadeite Orb
                      • You received a second request for the Jadeite Orb.
                      • Deliver the Jadeite Orb: Bring the Jadeite Orb to the individual who requested it (Everard/Offulve)

                       

                      Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This Quest has multiple Outcomes. You procured Two Jadeite Orbs and handed each to Everard and Offulve:

                      Two sought the Jadeite Orb, of which there is but one—or so it was thought. You, however, managed to procure a second Jadeite Orb, and thereby deliver both parties the genuine article, ensuring the satisfaction of everyone involved without any cause for deception.

                      You gave Everard the Jadeite Orb:

                      Two sought the Jadeite Orb, of which there is but one. You chose to earn the favor of the man of means, for it never hurts to be rewarded for your efforts.

                      You gave Offulve the Jadeite Orb:

                      Two sought the Jadeite Orb, of which there is but one. You chose to aid the man downtrodden, in the hope that he might find happiness in greener pastures:

                      You gave Everard a forgery, kept the Jadeite Orb for yourself, and promptly fled:

                      Two sought the Jadeite Orb, of which there is but one. Yet while you located this treasure at their behest, you were reluctant to part with it. Now you have claimed it as your own through cunning deception—but was it truly worth the price of the faith you have lost?

                      You procured Two Jadeite Orbs and handed each to Everard and Offulve - Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Rewards:

                      • 2,000 XP
                      • 12,000 G
                      • Ring of Skullduggery
                      • Elite Camping Kit

                       

                      You gave Offulve the Jadeite Orb - Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Rewards:

                      • 1,000 XP
                      • 3,000 G
                      • Elite Camping Kit

                       

                      You gave Everard a forgery and promptly fled - Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Rewards:

                      • x XP
                      • x Dcp
                      • Rewards

                       

                      Give Everard the Genuine Jadeite Orb - Hunt for the Jadeite Orb Rewards:

                      • 1000 XP
                      • 0 Dcp
                      • Ring of Skullduggery
                      • 12,000 G

                      Readvent of Calamity | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      Readvent of Calamity
                      Quest Location Melve
                      NPC Pickup n/a
                      Requirements Complete the Quests: Seat of the Sovran and progress Scaly Invaders
                      Time Limited Yes
                      Quest Canceled Feast of Deception
                      Unlocks Vocation: Mystic Spearhand
                      Other NPCs Sigurd, Ulrika, Lennart
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes: Repel or Felled the Dragon
                      Rewards 2,000 XP
                      Ring of Grit
                      4,500 G

                      Readvent of Calamity is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). The Readvent of Calamity is affected by the passage of time. When you visit Melve a few days after completing Seat of the Sovran and Scaly Invaders, a cutscene shows a dragon attacking the village. Hurry to repel the dragon before it flees.

                       

                      To suffer the dragon's attack once is a nightmare. To suffer it twice is nothing short of ill luck—and it seems Melve's fortunes are determined not to turn.

                       

                      How to Unlock Readvent of Calamity Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      This quest requires traveling to Melve at least twice and completing Seat of the Sovran and progressing Scaly Invaders. As you return to Melve, you will see a dragon attacking the town once again. See Readvent of Calamity on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Readvent of Calamity Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest
                      • This quest does not have multiple outcomes.
                      • This quest requires Seat of the Sovran, (you may need to complete at least 2 of Captain Brant's quests), and have visited Melve at least twice for this quest to start.
                      • This quest is discontinued if you don't finish it before the coronation quest, Feast of Deception, locking you out of any further quests in this questline.
                      • Some players have noted that Ulrika must be found first before progressing Scaly Invaders. This means that you must have progressed the first culling in Scaly Invaders but haven't fully completed it as the quest requires you to return to the area after the first part.
                      • Others have reported that Monster Culling (a quest from Brant) must be completed first and wait for a few days to visit Melve. This should trigger the dragon attack.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 Readvent of Calamity Quest Walkthrough

                      Repel the dragon

                      This quest starts after returning to Melve a few days after you have completed Seat of the Sovran and the Scaly Invaders. During our playthrough, we took the Oxcart from Vernworth to the village, and upon arriving, we saw a dragon attacking the town. Many of the townspeople are joining the fight to repel this dragon. Hurry to help Ulrika and the rest of the villagers before it flees.

                      The dragon has red bubbles on its body, particularly, the legs and neck areas. When hit, the dragon receives substantial damage, and breaking multiple of them will apply a stagger and possibly knock the dragon down. While knocked down, the dragon will lay on its side and you can land heavy attacks on its head or cast powerful spells to maximize your damage.

                      Warning: It is NOT recommended to kill the dragon here as it might complicate the process of obtaining the maister skill of Mystic Spearhand. In particular, the dialogue with Sigurd, the NPC that gives this skill scroll, might be locked to the version in Melve, so you might not be able to learn the skill until endgame.

                      Once the dragon's health drops to the 3rd bar, it will fly away and leave Melve for now. After the cutscene ends, you will be confronted by Ulrika, as she demands to reveal yourself to her. She will be thankful for your help and the man in red, and Lennart will butt in to ask Ulrika to return to the village as the townspeople are looking for her.

                      NOTE: There's an NPC named Sigurd that you can talk to in this area to unlock Mystic Spearhand. He will be shown for a brief moment while Ulrika extends her gratitude to both of you. In some cases, this dialogue will be automatically triggered. If not, just approach him and greet Sigurd. If you fell the dragon he will give you the reward Paladins Enigmata. 

                       

                      Alternate: Fell The Dragon

                      After the dragon's attack, a cut scene plays out. Run up to the watch tower and bring your pawns with the "To me" order. Once atop the tower engage the ballista. As you load it your pawns will come to your aid momentarily to move it. Carefully aim for the center chest of the dragon. I accomplished the kill by aiming above the stone arch near the middle of its raised wings if grounded. 

                      If the dragon attempts to fly off it will first raise off the ground. You have approximately 3 wing beats before it flies off to hit it with a ballista bolt. (2 attempts).

                      If successful Sigurd rewards you with the master teaching scroll as if you would slay the dragon later on. I also looted the weapon Dead Ringer (please confirm if guaranteed from this dragon or from this kill method). 

                       

                      Visit Melve from time to time 

                      After completing this, your next objective is to visit Melve now and then to check how things are faring after the dragon attack. You can travel to other towns and do other quests in the meantime. When you return, visit Ulrika's House to find her in a conversation with a man named Martin. He is delivering news from the Queen Regent and accuses Ulrika of treason. After that, she will ask you to stay the night.

                      If you alternatively Felled the dragon Martin will accuse ultimately of submitting a false report with alternative dialogue: "yet not only has the dragon been allowed to devastate the village, but in the wake of its attack you dared to submit what are clearly false reports of having failed the beast." "none save the Sovran can fell the dragon." Perchance you mistook an overgrown way even for the bringer of calamity". "The fault here lies with you, Ser Ulrika. You have failed in your duty as acting chief".

                      The following morning, Ulrika is nowhere to be found in her house. As you exit the premises, the man whom Ulrika was talking to before demands to find Ulrika, while Lennart stands behind him. Lennart announces that Ulrika has left in the dead of night, along with witnesses who can confirm it. Due to the absence of a leader, Martin will appoint themselves (a representative of the Queen Regent) as the village's leader. 

                      Once Martin leaves, Lennart will approach you to inform you what he knows about Ulrika. He believes that she hasn't truly abandoned the village and yet, he fears that it will be unwise for him to look for her, as he might draw suspicion from the troops. He asks that you inform him of Ulrika's well-being should you find her. 

                       

                      Find Ulrika and report to Lennart

                      Lennart believes that Ulrika may have left town to hunt the dragon. He wants you to look for her and while you don't have a marked objective for this quest, you will find Ulrika at the Harve Village, found southwest of Melve. If you haven't completed Trouble on the Cape, this quest will be triggered upon arriving at the location. After the quest, speak to Ulrika to progress this quest. 

                      Once you have learned about Ulrika's situation, return to Lennart in Melve, and inform him that Ulrika has taken up residence in Harve Village. The quest Readvent of Calamity will be marked as completed after this short conversation.

                       

                       

                      DD2 Readvent of Calamity Quest Objectives

                      • Repel the dragon
                      • Visit Melve from time to time
                      • Find Ulrika
                      • Inform Lennart of Ulrika's wellbeing

                       

                       

                      Readvent of Calamity Quest Rewards in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      You reported back to Lennart:

                      By virtue of its location, Melve has long served as the threshold of Vermund's defense against the dragon. Yet though a humble village can hardly be expected to repel such a beast without aid, the envoy from the capital bore not reinforcements, but accusations. It seems the queen regent intends to gain control of Melve by any means, leaving its leader, Ulrika, with little choice but to flee to protect her fellow villagers.

                      You reported back to Lennart - Readvent of Calamity Rewards:

                      • 2,000 XP
                      • Ring of Grit
                      • 4,500 G

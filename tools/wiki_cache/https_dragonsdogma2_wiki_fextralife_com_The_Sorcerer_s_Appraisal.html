                      The Sorcerer's Appraisal | Dragons Dogma 2 Wiki


                      The Sorcerer's Appraisal
                      Quest Location Myrddin's Home in Checkpoint Rest Town
                      NPC Pickup Myrddin
                      Requirements Wear Fancy Clothing
                      Time Limited No
                      Quest Canceled Unknown
                      Unlocks Maelstrom Skill for Sorcerers
                      Other NPCs Waldhar
                      Multiple Outcomes Yes
                      Rewards Myrddin's Chronicle (Maelstrom Skill)
                      Ares Morpho Robe
                      11000g

                      The Sorcerer's Appraisal is a Quest for Dragon's Dogma 2 (DD2). The Sorcerer's Appraisal is not affected by the passage of time and involves procuring several grimoires that Myrddin is missing. This serves as a test to prove to Myrddin that you are worthy of his secret knowledge.

                       

                      Residing in a manor in the checkpoint rest town is Myrddin, a sorcerer of great renown. He is even willing to share the secrets of his art—provided he finds you worthy, that is.

                       

                      How to Unlock The Sorcerer's Appraisal Quest in Dragon's Dogma 2

                      Put on Noble Clothing (Courtly Tunic, Courtly Breeches) and visit Myrddin in his house at Checkpoint Rest Town. Once you are inside, he will give you this quest. See The Sorcerer's Appraisal on the [Dragon's Dogma 2 Map].

                      If you are able to get within the home without wearing prestigious clothing, he will proceed to attack you.

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 The Sorcerer's Appraisal Quest Notes & Tips

                      • This quest does not unlock any other quest
                      • This quest has multiple outcomes
                      • This quest requires the player to be well-dressed
                      • This quest is canceled once you complete the postgame quest
                        Halls of the First Dawn
                      • You can get 3 books and give them to him at once in order to get the Myrddin's Chronicle which teaches you Maelstrom as well as 11000 gold and Ares Morpho Robe. This counts towards the Master of the Maistes gold trophy.

                       

                       

                      Dragon's Dogma 2 The Sorcerer's Appraisal Quest Walkthrough

                      Wear Noble Clothing to receive Myrddin's invite

                      Myrddin doesn't like the idea of mingling with the commoners and likes fancy items. Based on his preference, he would only invite noble people into his home, or those who wear noble clothing. During the early stages of the game, one of the ways that you can acquire noble clothing is to complete The Ornate Box, a side quest that you can complete to earn the gratitude of Sven, who will reward you with Courtly Breeches and Courtly Tunic upon completion.

                      Alternate: Buy the same noble clothing from Philbert's Sundries in Vernworth but they cost 150,000 G per piece.

                       

                      Gather the Grimoires

                      The first part of this quest involves procuring 3-5 Grimoires that Myrddin is looking for. This guide will indicate their locations and how to retrieve each tome. NOTE: There are two NPCs who seek the same tomes in Vermund. The other quest is called Spellbound, and a girl named Trysha seeks to harness her magick. Once you have collected enough tomes, it is possible to forge them by acquiring Ibrahim's service in Checkpoint Rest Town so you can decide if you want to give the originals to Trysha or Myrddin, or search for additional original tomes to satisfy both quests.

                      This quest is best completed after Spellbound, since this will positively affect this quest after you deliver the tomes.

                      Let There Be Light: Located in Myrddin's Home, found on the desk upstairs. When stealing this from Myrddin, go through the balcony from the outside but not up the stairs from the inside. If he catches you, he will attack you on the spot. 

                      Fulminous Shield: Purchased from Dudley in Melve [DD2 Map here]

                      Nation's Death Knell: Found in Ancient Battleground, located northeast of the Checkpoint Rest Town. The tome is found inside a chest located next to a cenotaph within the castle ruins (still part of the dungeon). Warning: In each playthrough there are only two original Nation's Death Knell. If you already gave both of them to Trysha and Myrdinn, the quest Tolled to Rest cannot be finished, which will also influence the outcome of Off the Pilfered Path. Therefore, it's better to finish Tolled to Rest before giving it to them.

                      Howling Blizzard: Wendy from the Nameless Village wants to exchange the Howling Blizzard for a Fulminous Shield. However, you don't have to give her the original, because the collection will be incomplete and will force you to abandon the quest. Instead, ask Ibrahim in Checkpoint Rest Town to forge the Fulminous Shield and give it to Wendy

                      Towering Earth: This can be acquired by speaking with Waldhar after he moves to The Gracious Hand's Vaults (see The Caged Magistrate and The Heel of History)

                       

                      Deliver the Grimoires

                      The best choice of the quests, The Sorcerer's Appraisal, Spellbound and Tolled to Rest is as follows:

                      1. Finish Tolled to Rest and then pick up Nation's Death Knell there.
                      2. (Optional) If you want to give Fulminous Shield to Wendy in Nameless Village, give her a forgery.
                      3. Give Trysha three original grimoires and finish Spellbound.
                      4. While equipped with Turquoise Ring, which Trysha gave you in Spellbound, give Myrddin three grimoires. It doesn't matter whether they are original or forged if you wear Turquoise Ring.

                      NOTE: Ibrahim is an NPC found in Checkpoint Rest Town. He can make forgeries for a fee, which is a copy of the original, but will not produce the same results as the original.

                       

                      DD2 The Sorcerer's Appraisal Quest Objectives

                      • Gather grimoires for Myrddin
                      • Prove your friendship with Trysha
                      • Revisit Myrddin in a few days' time
